# What the Monad?

This is a write-up about monads, something that I've been drawn to like a moth
to a flame for a long, _long_ time, and also probably _the_ thing that made me
fall in love with [Haskell](https://haskell.org). Haskell has been truly
eye-opening for me and has helped me most likely decide what I want to study in
life.

Monads are a big part of Haskell, but it took me a long while to understand what
they really are. In this write-up, I hope to share some of the revelations that
helped me understand this concept.
